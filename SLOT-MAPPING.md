## Retain/defer checks

- `resources.html` case covers remain unchanged: product screenshots cannot stand in for named-customer or sector case evidence.
- `company.html` team, vision, and partner visuals remain unchanged: product screenshots cannot represent people, strategy, or partners.
- `use-cases.html` sector illustrations remain unchanged: generic product UI does not establish industry-specific proof.
- Logos, social icons, P³ framework diagram, mobile visual, and CTA background remain unchanged.
- No screenshot with an “actual” label was used; all selected sources are confirmed demo data.
- No screenshot was cropped or redacted because the owner confirmed the archive contains demo data; this decision should be revisited before any future real-data archive is used.
